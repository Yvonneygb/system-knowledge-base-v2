<KbCard title="提交校验">
<ul><li>校验1：产品明细不能为空 —— 确保政策有实际产品内容</li><li><strong>详细逻辑</strong>：提交时校验产品明细列表(epmDiscountPolicyItemList)非空</li><li><strong>系统体现</strong>：阻断性报错</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：<code>SELECT COUNT(*) FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM WHERE DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = #&#123;discountPolicyId&#125;;</code></li></ul>
<ul><li>校验2：申请类型为产品(1)时产品编码必填 —— 确保产品类型申请有具体产品</li><li><strong>详细逻辑</strong>：applicationType=1时itemCode必填</li><li><strong>系统体现</strong>：前端DataSet dynamicProps required校验</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：<code>SELECT * FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM WHERE DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = #&#123;discountPolicyId&#125; AND APPLICATION_TYPE = 1 AND ITEM_CODE IS NULL;</code></li></ul>
<ul><li>校验3：申请类型为型号(2)时产品型号必填 —— 确保型号类型申请有具体型号</li><li><strong>详细逻辑</strong>：applicationType=2时itemModel必填</li><li><strong>系统体现</strong>：前端DataSet dynamicProps required校验</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：<code>SELECT * FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM WHERE DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = #&#123;discountPolicyId&#125; AND APPLICATION_TYPE = 2 AND ITEM_MODEL IS NULL;</code></li></ul>
<ul><li>校验4：单个经销商封顶数量须&lt;=政策封顶总数量行 —— 确保封顶数量逻辑合理</li><li><strong>详细逻辑</strong>：customerCapsNumber &gt; 0且totalCapNumber &gt; 0时，totalCapNumber须&gt;=customerCapsNumber</li><li><strong>系统体现</strong>：前端DataSet validator校验，提示"政策封顶总数量行必须大于单个经销商封顶数量"</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：<code>SELECT * FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM WHERE CUSTOMER_CAPS_NUMBER &gt; 0 AND TOTAL_CAP_NUMBER &gt; 0 AND TOTAL_CAP_NUMBER &lt; CUSTOMER_CAPS_NUMBER;</code></li></ul>
<ul><li>校验5：经销商封顶量须为正整数 —— 确保封顶量合法</li><li><strong>详细逻辑</strong>：cappingQty不能为空、不能为负数、须为整数</li><li><strong>系统体现</strong>：前端DataSet validator校验，提示"请输入经销商封顶量"/"请输入整数"/"经销商封顶量不能为负数"</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：<code>SELECT * FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY_CUSTOMER WHERE CAPPING_QTY IS NULL OR CAPPING_QTY &lt; 0;</code></li></ul>

<h4>报错1：政策名称不能为空</h4>
<ul><li><strong>触发条件</strong>：详情页点击"保存"按钮时，政策名称（DISCOUNT_POLICY_NAME）字段为空</li><li><strong>逻辑分析</strong>：前端校验discountPolicyName非空，或后端实体注解校验失败。政策名称是折扣政策的唯一标识，最大长度30字符，必填。</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：</li></ul>
<pre class="detail-sql" v-pre><code>SELECT P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID AS 政策ID,
         P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_CODE AS 政策编号,
         P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_NAME AS 政策名称,
         P.HZ_APPROVE_STATUS AS 审核状态
  FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY P
  WHERE P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = :policyId
    AND P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_NAME IS NULL;</code></pre>
<h4>报错2：有效结束日期须大于有效开始日期</h4>
<ul><li><strong>触发条件</strong>：详情页保存时，有效结束日期（EFFECTIVE_DATE_END）早于或等于有效开始日期（EFFECTIVE_DATE_START）</li><li><strong>逻辑分析</strong>：前端日期选择器联动校验effectiveDateEnd &gt; effectiveDateStart，或后端校验失败。折扣政策需有明确且合法的有效期范围，结束日期必须大于开始日期。</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：</li></ul>
<pre class="detail-sql" v-pre><code>SELECT P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_CODE AS 政策编号,
         P.EFFECTIVE_DATE_START AS 有效开始日期,
         P.EFFECTIVE_DATE_END AS 有效结束日期
  FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY P
  WHERE P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = :policyId
    AND P.EFFECTIVE_DATE_END &lt;= P.EFFECTIVE_DATE_START;</code></pre>
<h4>报错3：折扣政策名称最大输入30个字符</h4>
<ul><li><strong>触发条件</strong>：详情页保存时，政策名称（DISCOUNT_POLICY_NAME）长度超过30个字符</li><li><strong>逻辑分析</strong>：后端EpmDiscountPolicyServiceImpl校验折扣政策名称长度，超过30字符时抛出"折扣政策名称最大输入30个字符"。数据库字段DISCOUNT_POLICY_NAME为VARCHAR(30)。</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：</li></ul>
<pre class="detail-sql" v-pre><code>SELECT P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_CODE AS 政策编号,
         P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_NAME AS 政策名称,
         LENGTH(P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_NAME) AS 名称长度
  FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY P
  WHERE P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = :policyId
    AND LENGTH(P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_NAME) &gt; 30;</code></pre>
<h4>报错4：产品明细不能为空</h4>
<ul><li><strong>触发条件</strong>：详情页点击"提交"按钮时，产品明细列表（lineDs）为空</li><li><strong>逻辑分析</strong>：前端handleSubmit校验lineDs.toData().length为0时提示"还未维护产品明细!"。后端EpmDiscountPolicyServiceImpl也校验产品行不能为空。提交前必须至少维护一条产品明细。</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：</li></ul>
<pre class="detail-sql" v-pre><code>SELECT P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_CODE AS 政策编号,
         (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM I
          WHERE I.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID) AS 产品明细数量
  FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY P
  WHERE P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = :policyId;</code></pre>
<h4>报错5：至少保留一条有效产品明细行</h4>
<ul><li><strong>触发条件</strong>：详情页保存时，所有产品明细行被删除（_status为delete）</li><li><strong>逻辑分析</strong>：前端handleValidator过滤非删除状态记录（validLineList），若为空则提示"至少保留一条有效产品明细行"。防止用户删除所有明细后保存空单据。</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：</li></ul>
<pre class="detail-sql" v-pre><code>SELECT P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_CODE AS 政策编号,
         (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM I
          WHERE I.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID
            AND I.VALID_STAT &lt;&gt; 3) AS 有效明细数量
  FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY P
  WHERE P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = :policyId;</code></pre>
<h4>报错6：政策封顶总数量行必须大于单个经销商封顶数量</h4>
<ul><li><strong>触发条件</strong>：保存产品明细时，capping=2（校验单个经销商封顶）且customerCapsNumber &gt; totalCapNumber</li><li><strong>逻辑分析</strong>：前端LineDS的validator校验customerCapsNumber &gt; 0且totalCapNumber &gt; 0时，totalCapNumber须&gt;=customerCapsNumber。后端EpmDiscountPolicyServiceImpl也校验该逻辑。确保封顶数量逻辑合理。</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：</li></ul>
<pre class="detail-sql" v-pre><code>SELECT I.ITEM_CODE AS 产品编码,
         I.TOTAL_CAP_NUMBER AS 政策封顶总数量,
         I.CUSTOMER_CAPS_NUMBER AS 单个经销商封顶数量
  FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM I
  WHERE I.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = :policyId
    AND I.CAPPING = 2
    AND I.CUSTOMER_CAPS_NUMBER &gt; 0
    AND I.TOTAL_CAP_NUMBER &gt; 0
    AND I.TOTAL_CAP_NUMBER &lt; I.CUSTOMER_CAPS_NUMBER;</code></pre>
<h4>报错7：单个经销商封顶数量为正整数</h4>
<ul><li><strong>触发条件</strong>：保存产品明细时，capping=2且customerCapsNumber为空、负数或非整数</li><li><strong>逻辑分析</strong>：前端ToppingOutDS的validator校验cappingQty不能为空、不能为负数、须为整数。后端EpmDiscountPolicyServiceImpl校验"单个经销商封顶数量为正整数"。</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：</li></ul>
<pre class="detail-sql" v-pre><code>SELECT C.CUSTOMER_CODE AS 经销商编码,
         C.CUSTOMER_NAME AS 经销商名称
  FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY_CUSTOMER C
  WHERE C.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = :policyId
    AND (C.CAPPING_QTY IS NULL OR C.CAPPING_QTY &lt; 0 OR C.CAPPING_QTY &lt;&gt; FLOOR(C.CAPPING_QTY));</code></pre>
<h4>报错8：起订量需小于封顶量</h4>
<ul><li><strong>触发条件</strong>：阶梯政策行编辑起订量（minimumQty）时，值大于封顶量（cappingQty）</li><li><strong>逻辑分析</strong>：前端监听infoDetailDs的update事件，当name=='minimumQty'且value &gt; record.get('cappingQty')时回滚值并提示"起订量需小于封顶量"。确保阶梯政策区间合法。</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：</li></ul>
<pre class="detail-sql" v-pre><code>SELECT I.ITEM_CODE AS 产品编码,
         L.MINIMUM_QTY AS 起订量,
         L.CAPPING_QTY AS 封顶量
  FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM_LINE L
    INNER JOIN EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM I ON I.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM_ID = L.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM_ID
  WHERE I.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = :policyId
    AND L.MINIMUM_QTY &gt; 0
    AND L.CAPPING_QTY &gt; 0
    AND L.MINIMUM_QTY &gt;= L.CAPPING_QTY;</code></pre>
<h4>报错9：阶梯政策区间冲突</h4>
<ul><li><strong>触发条件</strong>：阶梯政策行编辑起订量或封顶量时，与同一产品明细下其他阶梯政策行区间重叠</li><li><strong>逻辑分析</strong>：前端监听infoDetailDs的update事件，循环比对当前行与其他行的起订量-封顶量区间。若存在重叠（A&lt;=C &amp;&amp; B&gt;=C 等四种情况）则回滚值并提示"起订量与封顶量的区间与第N行阶梯政策区间冲突"。</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：</li></ul>
<pre class="detail-sql" v-pre><code>SELECT A.ITEM_CODE AS 产品编码,
         A.MINIMUM_QTY AS 行A起订量, A.CAPPING_QTY AS 行A封顶量,
         B.MINIMUM_QTY AS 行B起订量, B.CAPPING_QTY AS 行B封顶量
  FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM_LINE A
    INNER JOIN EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM_LINE B
      ON B.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM_ID = A.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM_ID
     AND B.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM_LINE_ID &lt;&gt; A.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM_LINE_ID
    INNER JOIN EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM I ON I.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM_ID = A.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM_ID
  WHERE I.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = :policyId
    AND A.MINIMUM_QTY &lt;= B.CAPPING_QTY
    AND A.CAPPING_QTY &gt;= B.MINIMUM_QTY;</code></pre>
<h4>报错10：折扣率未填写</h4>
<ul><li><strong>触发条件</strong>：保存时，优惠方式为折扣（preferentialType=1）的阶梯政策行折扣率（discountRate）为空</li><li><strong>逻辑分析</strong>：前端handleValidator遍历产品明细，对preferentialType==1的行校验每条阶梯政策的discountRate非空。若为空则提示"第X的第Y条明细的折扣率未填写！"。</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：</li></ul>
<pre class="detail-sql" v-pre><code>SELECT I.ITEM_CODE AS 产品编码,
         I.PREFERENTIAL_TYPE AS 优惠方式,
         L.MINIMUM_QTY AS 起订量,
         L.DISCOUNT_RATE AS 折扣率
  FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM_LINE L
    INNER JOIN EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM I ON I.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM_ID = L.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM_ID
  WHERE I.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = :policyId
    AND I.PREFERENTIAL_TYPE = 1
    AND L.DISCOUNT_RATE IS NULL;</code></pre>
<h4>报错11：请先选择销售渠道/政策类型/币种</h4>
<ul><li><strong>触发条件</strong>：详情页点击"新增"产品明细或阶梯政策时，头信息的channel、policyType或currency为空</li><li><strong>逻辑分析</strong>：前端handleLineCreate和handleInfoCreate校验头信息channel、policyType、currency非空。若任一为空则提示"请先选择销售渠道/政策类型/币种"。这些字段是产品明细和阶梯政策的前置依赖。</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：</li></ul>
<pre class="detail-sql" v-pre><code>SELECT P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_CODE AS 政策编号,
         P.CHANNEL AS 销售渠道,
         P.POLICY_TYPE AS 政策类型,
         P.CURRENCY AS 币种
  FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY P
  WHERE P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = :policyId
    AND (P.CHANNEL IS NULL OR P.POLICY_TYPE IS NULL OR P.CURRENCY IS NULL);</code></pre>
<h4>报错12：批次开始时间和批次结束时间必须同时填写</h4>
<ul><li><strong>触发条件</strong>：详情页保存时，批次开始日期（BATCH_START_DATE）和批次结束日期（BATCH_END_DATE）只有一个有值</li><li><strong>逻辑分析</strong>：前端checkValidity校验(batchStartDate &amp;&amp; !batchEndDate) || (!batchStartDate &amp;&amp; batchEndDate)，若成立则提示"批次开始时间和批次结束时间必须同时填写或同时为空"。</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：</li></ul>
<pre class="detail-sql" v-pre><code>SELECT P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_CODE AS 政策编号,
         P.BATCH_START_DATE AS 批次开始日期,
         P.BATCH_END_DATE AS 批次结束日期
  FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY P
  WHERE P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = :policyId
    AND (
      (P.BATCH_START_DATE IS NOT NULL AND P.BATCH_END_DATE IS NULL)
      OR (P.BATCH_START_DATE IS NULL AND P.BATCH_END_DATE IS NOT NULL)
    );</code></pre>
<h4>报错13：仅支持.xlsx、.xls格式文件</h4>
<ul><li><strong>触发条件</strong>：详情页产品明细导入时，选择的文件不是.xlsx或.xls格式</li><li><strong>逻辑分析</strong>：前端handleImportProduct校验文件后缀名，若不是.xlsx或.xls则提示"仅支持 .xlsx、.xls 格式文件"并阻断上传。</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：</li></ul>
<pre class="detail-sql" v-pre><code>-- 文件格式校验为前端校验，无对应数据查询
  -- 可检查导入历史记录
  SELECT P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_CODE AS 政策编号, P.CREATION_DATE AS 创建时间
  FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY P
  WHERE P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = :policyId;</code></pre>
<h4>报错14：导入的产品编码查询不到对应的产品信息</h4>
<ul><li><strong>触发条件</strong>：产品明细导入时，Excel中的产品编码在系统中不存在</li><li><strong>逻辑分析</strong>：后端EpmDiscountPolicyServiceImpl的importProduct方法校验导入的产品编码，若查询不到对应的产品信息则抛出"导入的产品编码查询不到对应的产品信息：[编码列表]"。</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：</li></ul>
<pre class="detail-sql" v-pre><code>SELECT I.ITEM_CODE AS 导入产品编码
  FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM I
  WHERE I.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = :policyId
    AND NOT EXISTS (
      SELECT 1 FROM LNK_PROD P WHERE P.ITEM_CODE = I.ITEM_CODE
    );</code></pre>
<h4>报错15：提交前请先获取零售折扣底限</h4>
<ul><li><strong>触发条件</strong>：详情页点击"提交"按钮时，存在申请类型为产品（applicationType=1）的明细但prodAttributionChannel为空</li><li><strong>逻辑分析</strong>：前端handleSubmit校验hasProduct（存在applicationType==1的行）且prodAttributionChannel为空时提示"提交前请先获取零售折扣底限！"。需先点击"获取实时价格"按钮获取零售折扣底限。</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：</li></ul>
<pre class="detail-sql" v-pre><code>SELECT P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_CODE AS 政策编号,
         P.PROD_ATTRIBUTION_CHANNEL AS 品类,
         (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY_ITEM I
          WHERE I.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID
            AND I.APPLICATION_TYPE = 1) AS 产品类型明细数
  FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY P
  WHERE P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = :policyId
    AND P.PROD_ATTRIBUTION_CHANNEL IS NULL;</code></pre>
<h4>报错16：未找到该单据</h4>
<ul><li><strong>触发条件</strong>：详情页删除或编辑折扣政策时，根据discountPolicyId未查询到记录</li><li><strong>逻辑分析</strong>：后端EpmDiscountPolicyServiceImpl根据discountPolicyId查询EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY，若不存在则抛出"未找到该单据"。通常由记录被他人删除或使用了缓存的ID导致。</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：</li></ul>
<pre class="detail-sql" v-pre><code>SELECT P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID AS 政策ID,
         P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_CODE AS 政策编号,
         P.HZ_APPROVE_STATUS AS 审核状态
  FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY P
  WHERE P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = :policyId;</code></pre>
<h4>报错17：仅新建状态单据允许删除</h4>
<ul><li><strong>触发条件</strong>：详情页点击"删除"按钮时，单据状态（HZ_APPROVE_STATUS）不是NEW</li><li><strong>逻辑分析</strong>：后端EpmDiscountPolicyServiceImpl校验单据状态，非NEW状态抛出"仅新建状态单据允许删除."。防止删除已提交或已审核的政策。</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：</li></ul>
<pre class="detail-sql" v-pre><code>SELECT P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_CODE AS 政策编号,
         P.HZ_APPROVE_STATUS AS 审核状态
  FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY P
  WHERE P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_ID = :policyId
    AND P.HZ_APPROVE_STATUS &lt;&gt; 'NEW';</code></pre>
<h4>报错18：暂无数据</h4>
<ul><li><strong>触发条件</strong>：列表页查询时，查询条件无匹配的折扣政策记录</li><li><strong>逻辑分析</strong>：前端列表DataSet查询返回空集合时框架提示"暂无数据"。通常由查询条件过严、数据未生成或权限不足导致。</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：</li></ul>
<pre class="detail-sql" v-pre><code>SELECT COUNT(*) AS 记录数
  FROM EPM_DISCOUNT_POLICY P
  WHERE P.HZ_APPROVE_STATUS = NVL(:status, P.HZ_APPROVE_STATUS)
    AND P.DISCOUNT_POLICY_NAME LIKE NVL(:policyName, '%')
    AND P.CREATION_DATE BETWEEN NVL(:dateFrom, P.CREATION_DATE) AND NVL(:dateTo, P.CREATION_DATE);</code></pre>
<h4>报错19：会话过期</h4>
<ul><li><strong>触发条件</strong>：任意操作时，用户登录态失效（token过期或被踢出）</li><li><strong>逻辑分析</strong>：前端请求拦截器检测到响应401状态码时提示"会话过期"并跳转登录页。通常由长时间未操作、多端登录或服务端session过期导致。</li><li><strong>排查SQL</strong>：</li></ul>
<pre class="detail-sql" v-pre><code>-- 会话过期为前端拦截，无对应数据查询
  -- 可检查用户最近登录时间
  SELECT U.USER_ID AS 用户ID, U.LOGIN_NAME AS 登录名,
         U.LAST_LOGIN_DATE AS 最近登录时间
  FROM HPFM_USER U
  WHERE U.USER_ID = :userId;</code></pre>
